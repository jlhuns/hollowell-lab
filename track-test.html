Tracking test

  1. Use the room and lighting you'll play in.
  2. Tap Start, allow motion and camera. The camera is used for tracking only and is never shown.
  3. Move the phone slowly left and right until it says Tracking.
  4. Point at the floor a step ahead and tap Set floor.
  5. Walk 3–5 m, turn round and walk back to where you started. Watch the map: a good result ends near the start dot.

Uses AlvaAR (open source, GPL-3.0) for camera tracking.
